## The rules these produce

**1. The opening line takes an exclamation point.**
Three posts in a row now: the Gemini post, Etched (`In July it was $10.3B!`) and
the teen launch (`...version of ChatGPT!`). He puts it either on the first line or
on the most surprising number.

**2. Delete the interpretive nudge.** This is the biggest one. The machine states
a fact and then adds a line telling the reader what to make of it. He cut every
single one:

| Cut | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `That's not a round, that's a re-rating.` | aphorism about the fact above it |
| `which is its own tell` | tells the reader the fact is significant |
| `This isn't a deck.` | second aphorism, same paragraph |
| `The part I keep coming back to is the orders.` | writerly framing before a fact |
| `Here's the context that makes it interesting--` | same move, different post |

Facts sit next to each other and the reader does the work. Save the interpretation
for the last line.

**3. Stack the facts into one paragraph.** He merged the $21B paragraph and the
$1B-in-orders paragraph into a single run: raise, valuation, prior valuation,
orders, shipping. Related numbers belong together, not spread over three
paragraphs with commentary between them.

**4. De-dramatize the verbs and nouns.** `hard blocks on` became `restrictions
on`. `Meta is walking into a trial` became `Meta is on trial`. The plainer word,
every time.

**5. A rhetorical question is the take wearing a disguise. Cut it.**
He deleted both closing questions and replaced them with statements:

- `Anybody else think inference is where this actually gets decided?` →
  `Models = commodities.  Inference is where the alpha comes from.`
- `Anybody else reading this as legal strategy more than product?` → deleted, the
  post ends on `Smart move, actually!`

Note what he *kept*, in the two posts he did not touch:
`Anybody comfortable with that? I'm genuinely not sure I am.` and
`Anybody actually shipping on open models in production, or is it still mostly
experiments?`

**The test:** does the question ask something you actually want to know? If it
only restates the thesis with a question mark on the end (`Anybody else think
[my argument]?`), cut it and state the argument. If it asks the room for
experience you don't have, keep it.

**6. Endings compress hard, and can use shorthand.** `Models = commodities.` An
equals sign in a LinkedIn post. Finance vocabulary too — `alpha`. When he sharpens
an ending it gets shorter and more clipped, not longer.
