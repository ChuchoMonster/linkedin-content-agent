Snowflake's growth accelerated to 37% last quarter and the stock jumped 17%!

Up from 30% at the end of last year, and management says AI products were about half of that acceleration. Its coding agent CoCo is in 9,100 accounts now, up more than 2,000 in the quarter.

On the same call they cut full-year product gross margin guidance from 75% to 74%, because AI workloads carry a lower contribution margin.

They raised the full-year operating margin guide anyway. 334 people added so far this year, against 935 in the same stretch last year.

AI revenue is real revenue, it just costs more to deliver. The hiring line is where they found the difference.
