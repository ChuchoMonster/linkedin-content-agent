Nvidia is guaranteeing $105B in financing so OpenAI can buy Nvidia chips!

It's for a data center campus in Pike County, Ohio. SoftBank's SB Energy builds and runs it, OpenAI leases it for 20 years, and Nvidia is putting another $1.5B into SB Energy.

OpenAI committed to using Nvidia chips exclusively onsite. Up to 8 gigawatts, with the first 800MW live in 2028.

Vendor financing is an old move and it has gone badly before, but the demand here is real today and the chips do get used.

When the supplier is funding the buyer, the order book and the loan book are the same book.
