White collar jobs aren't the ones at risk, blue collar jobs are!

The US added 162,000 jobs in August against 53,000 expected, and unemployment held at 4.1%. Information jobs shrank while leisure and hospitality added 62,000.

So jobs are moving around the economy, not vanishing. Displacement, not destruction.

Most white collar work has a person on the other end of it. When you hire an RIA you want the human touch, and AI just makes that advisor faster.

When you hire a house painter you just want your house painted. Nobody's paying extra for the relationship.

Robots aren't painting houses at scale yet, and the timeline is anyone's guess. But when they are, the painter has nothing to fall back on.

The white collar panic is aimed at the wrong collar.
