Three AI labs shipped models in two days and competed only on price!

Anthropic put out Opus 5.5 on Tuesday at $4 in / $20 out per million tokens, down 20%. About 90 minutes later OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Sol at $2 / $10, a straight 50% cut, and called the new rate permanent rather than promotional.

Grok 4.7 landed the day before, also at $2 on input.

OpenAI says the cut just reflects cheaper inference, and that's probably true. But you don't make a price permanent unless you expect the other guy to match it.

Same product, three suppliers, and the announcement is the discount. That's a commodity, and I say that as someone paying $200/mo for Claude.

Commodity producers don't make their money on the commodity, they make it downstream. So the labs have to go find margin in somebody else's industry.

Whatever business you're in, one of these companies is eventually going to show up in it.
