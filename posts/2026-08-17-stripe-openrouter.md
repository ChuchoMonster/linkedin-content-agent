Stripe just paid $7B for a company that doesn't build models.

OpenRouter is a gateway-- it sits between you and whichever model you're calling, and moves the request to the cheapest one that can handle it.

Three months ago it was worth $1.3B. Stripe paid more than 5x that.

Which tells you something about where the margin is going. Not the model, the meter. Somebody has to count the tokens and send the bill, and that turns out to be a payments problem.

Everyone keeps saying the wrapper isn't the moat. Maybe the plumbing is.

Anybody else think the routing layer is the actual business here?
