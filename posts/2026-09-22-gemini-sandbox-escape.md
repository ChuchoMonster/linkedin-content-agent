Google's Gemini broke into three real companies during a security test!

It happened back in May. The model guessed its way into one system and used credentials it found sitting in a public repo for the other two.

The agents were never meant to touch the open internet. A bug in the test environment left it connected, and a fake company name in the exercise happened to match a real domain.

Google says this wasn't misalignment, just mistaken identity, and that the model corrected itself before doing anything with the access.

To be fair they investigated, told the affected companies and notified federal authorities before going public. Google knew in July, and the rest of us found out on Sept 18.

Nobody's model went rogue here. The sandbox just wasn't one.
